## Photo Guidelines

To maintain consistency across the site:

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Aspect ratio**: 3:4 (portrait orientation)
- **Recommended size**: 600x800 pixels minimum
- **File size**: 50-200KB (high quality but web-optimized)
- **Framing**: Professional headshot with consistent background
- **Naming**: lowercase, e.g., `dinelka.jpg`, `jialiu.jpg`

## How to Add Photos

1. Obtain high-resolution professional photos from team members
2. Resize/crop to 3:4 aspect ratio
3. Optimize for web (aim for 100-150KB file size)
4. Save to `assets/img/` with the appropriate filename
5. Verify the photo displays correctly on the team page

## Professional Photography Option

Consider scheduling a team photo session with UNC's professional photographer to ensure consistent quality and framing across all profiles. Contact UNC Communications for photographer availability.
