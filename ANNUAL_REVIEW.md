## Overview

This annual review is a **strategic assessment** of your website's effectiveness, alignment with career goals, and competitive positioning. Unlike quarterly audits (which focus on content updates), this review asks **bigger questions** about direction, structure, and impact.

**Purpose**:
- Ensure website aligns with current research priorities
- Assess effectiveness in achieving goals (recruitment, collaboration, visibility)
- Identify areas for improvement based on data
- Plan strategic updates for the coming year

## Questions to Reflect On

Use these prompts for deeper strategic thinking:

**Research Identity**:
- What do I want to be known for in 5 years?
- How does my website support that identity?
- What narrative thread connects my work?

**Audience**:
- Who visits my website most? (students, collaborators, press)
- Am I serving my target audience well?
- Should I segment content by audience?

**Impact**:
- What has been the ROI of website maintenance?
- Has it helped achieve career goals?
- Is it worth the time investment? (if not, scale back)

**Evolution**:
- How has my research changed in the past year?
- Does the website reflect that evolution?
- What legacy am I building?
