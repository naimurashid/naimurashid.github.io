Euphy Wu completed her PhD in Biostatistics at UNC under the guidance of Dr. Naim Rashid and [Dr. Mike Love](https://mikelove.github.io/). Her research focused on statistical genetics, developing methods for detecting allelic imbalance at the isoform level and single-cell clustering with topic models. She holds a BS in Mathematics and Integrative Biology from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.
