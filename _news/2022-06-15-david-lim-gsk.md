Congratulations to **David Lim** on successfully defending his PhD in Biostatistics! David's dissertation, mentored by Dr. Naim Rashid, developed deep learning frameworks for non-ignorable missing data (dlGLM, NIMIWAE) and feature-specific clustering (FSCseq), producing three first-author publications. He joined GSK as a Principal Statistician and is now a Research Scientist at Google.
