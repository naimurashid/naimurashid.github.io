Dr. Rashid presented "Staying in the Driver's Seat: Calibrating AI Use in Statistical Research, Training, and Practice" as part of the American Statistical Association's StatsUp.AI webinar series. The talk laid out practical guardrails and habits for using AI tools in statistical work while keeping scientific ownership. [Slides](https://rashidlab.github.io/talks/2026-04-25-statsupai-staying-in-drivers-seat-deck/staying-in-drivers-seat-v3.html) and a [companion resources repository](https://github.com/naimurashid/staying-in-drivers-seat-resources) with templates, checklists, and the full lab guide are freely available.
