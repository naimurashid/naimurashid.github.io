  <h2 class="page-intro__title">Research Vision</h2>
  <p class="page-intro__lede">
    The Rashid Lab develops statistical methods and computational tools that bridge genomics, clinical trials, and precision medicine.
  </p>
  <p class="mb-0">
    Our portfolio spans four interconnected research themes, each pairing methodological advances with translational applications at UNC Lineberger Comprehensive Cancer Center.
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>Precision medicine engines for biomarker-driven trials</li>
    <li>Transcriptomic + epigenomic software for regulatory discovery</li>
    <li>Generative AI for missing data, imaging, and adaptive monitoring</li>
    <li>Trial innovation + mentorship to move methods into practice</li>
  </ul>
</section>

## Research Themes

<div class="research-themes">
  <div class="research-themes__grid">

    <div class="research-theme research-theme--primary">
      <div class="research-theme__icon">🎯</div>
      <h3>Cancer Precision Medicine</h3>
      <p class="research-theme__description">
        Statistical engines for biomarker-driven treatment selection, subtyping, and patient stratification.
      </p>
      <ul class="theme-list">
        <li>PurIST single-sample classifier powering PDAC trials.</li>
        <li>Stroma-aware subtyping and multi-omic GLMMs across cancers.</li>
        <li>Between-study reproducibility frameworks for biomarkers.</li>
      </ul>
      <a href="/publications/?filter=precision-medicine" class="btn btn-sm btn-outline-primary">View Publications</a>
    </div>

    <div class="research-theme research-theme--secondary">
      <div class="research-theme__icon">🧬</div>
      <h3>Transcriptomic, Epigenomic, and Bioinformatics Tool Development</h3>
      <p class="research-theme__description">
        Open-source software for RNA-seq, ChIP/ATAC, and multi-omic discovery across regulatory programs.
      </p>
      <ul class="theme-list">
        <li>CompDTUReg, FSCseq, and related RNA-seq toolkits.</li>
        <li>epigraHMM/mixNBHMM for multi-condition enrichment.</li>
        <li>Allele-specific and isoform-level inference pipelines.</li>
      </ul>
      <a href="/publications/?filter=genomics" class="btn btn-sm btn-outline-primary">View Publications</a>
    </div>

    <div class="research-theme research-theme--tertiary">
      <div class="research-theme__icon">🤖</div>
      <h3>Generative AI and Deep Learning</h3>
      <p class="research-theme__description">
        Generative and deep-learning architectures for missing data, semi-supervised learning, and computational pathology.
      </p>
      <ul class="theme-list">
        <li>NIMIWAE and dlglm for non-ignorable missingness.</li>
        <li>Semi-supervised matrix factorization for subtyping.</li>
        <li>Generative copilots for trial matching and EHR analytics.</li>
      </ul>
      <a href="/publications/?filter=machine-learning" class="btn btn-sm btn-outline-primary">View Publications</a>
    </div>

    <div class="research-theme research-theme--quaternary">
      <div class="research-theme__icon">🏥</div>
      <h3>Adaptive Trial Design & Real-Time Biomarker Integration</h3>
      <p class="research-theme__description">
        Bayesian platform designs that ingest ctDNA, imaging, and clinical signals to guide oncology decisions.
      </p>
      <ul class="theme-list">
        <li>ARPA-H ADAPT and TBCRC evolutionary designs.</li>
        <li>Biomarker-aware randomization with serial ctDNA.</li>
        <li>Master protocols for cooperative group studies.</li>
      </ul>
      <a href="/publications/?filter=trials" class="btn btn-sm btn-outline-primary">View Publications</a>
