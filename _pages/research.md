      <div class="focus-card__chips">
        <span class="focus-chip">PurIST subtype briefs for GI tumor boards</span>

    <article class="focus-card">

      <h3>Transcriptomic and epigenomic software</h3>
      <p>Open-source RNA-seq and chromatin pipelines powering Lineberger, TCGA, and SPORE discovery efforts.</p>
      <div class="focus-card__chips">
        <span class="focus-chip">CompDTUReg for isoform-level RNA testing</span>
        <span class="focus-chip">epigraHMM + mixNBHMM for multi-condition enrichment</span>

      </div>
      <a href="/software/" class="link-arrow">Browse toolkits →</a>
    </article>

    <article class="focus-card">
      <p class="focus-card__badge">AI &amp; deep learning</p>
      <h3>Generative copilots and missing-data engines</h3>
      <p>Deep learning, LLM, and probabilistic models that rescue incomplete data and recommend trial actions.</p>
      <div class="focus-card__chips">
        <span class="focus-chip">NIMIWAE + dlGLM for non-ignorable missingness</span>
        <span class="focus-chip">Semi-supervised factorization for cancer subtyping</span>
        <span class="focus-chip">LLM copilots for trial matching and ctDNA monitoring</span>
      </div>
      <a href="/publications/?filter=machine-learning" class="link-arrow">Machine learning work →</a>
    </article>

    <article class="focus-card">
      <p class="focus-card__badge">Trial innovation</p>

      <p>Bayesian platforms and IQS pipelines that ingest ctDNA, imaging, and clinical signals inside cooperative trials.</p>
