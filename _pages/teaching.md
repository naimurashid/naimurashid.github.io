## Teaching Snapshot

### BIOS 667: Applied Longitudinal Data Analysis (current)
- Core graduate course (3 credits, ~25 students).
- GEEs, mixed models, joint modeling, and missing-data tactics tied to oncology case studies.
- Weekly Canvas modules with R Markdown labs + SAS templates keep workflows reproducible.

- BIOS 735 Statistical Computing (2019–2024): tidyverse, git/GitHub, simulation, optimization, and package development. [Course site](https://biodatascience.github.io/statcomp/)
- BIOS 663 (2016–2018): intermediate linear models with emphasis on diagnostics, mixed models, and design decisions for biomedical data.
</section>

<section class="page-section" markdown="1">
## Teaching Philosophy

Pair rigorous theory with hands-on tooling. Every course anchors to collaborative datasets, version control, and open documentation so students can move seamlessly between Gillings, Lineberger, and industry teams.
</section>
